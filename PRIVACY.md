# Privacy policy

Effective: 2026-11-04. Project: Notice Sender / 공지발송, maintained by [SleepingDawn](https://github.com/SleepingDawn).

## Data handled on the operator's computer

The desktop app manages information selected or entered by its operator: student names, schools, identifiers, chatroom names, classes, notice templates, lesson results, attachment paths, settings and delivery logs. It stores application data locally and can import/export local CSV, XLSX and JSON files. Windows uses a per-user application-data directory. Exports, backups, attachment folders and diagnostic reports remain under the operator's control and can contain personal information.

The current Windows application has no project-operated network backend, advertising or analytics client. It does not upload its student database or diagnostics to the maintainer or SignPath. The project does not ask for KakaoTalk passwords; the operator logs into KakaoTalk itself. It does not log into Google Sheets or call its API; spreadsheet values are imported from files or pasted by the operator.

## Requested transfers and other services

When the operator authorizes sending, selected message text and files are delivered through the locally installed KakaoTalk client to selected recipients. KakaoTalk and its recipients then process that content under their own arrangements. See [Kakao's privacy policy](https://www.kakao.com/policy/privacy). A dry run searches/opens/verifies rooms without entering or sending message text or attachments.

The macOS app has an optional Apple Intelligence preset editor using Apple's system framework. Its availability and processing are controlled by macOS and Apple; see [Apple's privacy information](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). This feature is not present in the Windows port.

Downloading releases or interacting with the repository is subject to [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Browser and operating-system services have their own policies. The app does not automatically submit private support reports. Information deliberately posted in public issues or uploaded elsewhere becomes subject to that destination's handling.

## Operator controls

The operator chooses imports, recipients, exports and attachments and can remove local records/files. Deleting a local record does not recall an already sent message or remove separately exported backups. Keep student data and private reports out of public issues and release artifacts. For project questions, use the repository's issue tracker without posting personal records.
